## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-11
### Initial Release
- Comprehensive psychoeducational self-reflection tool based on the WHO ICD-11 dimensional trait-and-severity model.
- 30-item self-reflection assessment covering the 5 ICD-11 trait domains (Negative Affectivity, Detachment, Dissociality, Disinhibition, Anankastia) plus Borderline pattern specifier.
- Caregiver Hub with practical communication scripts, healthy boundaries without guilt, and family support resources.
- Verified national crisis helplines (Tele-MANAS 14416, 112, KIRAN, Vandrevala Foundation) with direct one-tap dialing.
- Trilingual support in English, Hindi, and Gujarati with zero network requests and 100% on-device privacy.
- Clean, colorful tile interface designed for calm and stigma-free reflection.
